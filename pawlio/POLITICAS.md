## Refund policy

**The Happy Dog Guarantee — 60 days**

We want you and your dog to love Pawlio. If you're not completely happy, email
us at [support@pawlio.com] within 60 days of delivery and we'll refund your
purchase. No need to send the product back.

**How to request a refund**
1. Email [support@pawlio.com] with your order number.
2. Tell us briefly what didn't work for you (it helps us improve).
3. We'll process your refund to the original payment method within 5 business
   days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

**Subscriptions**
The guarantee covers your first subscription order. You can cancel future
deliveries anytime from your account — see our Subscription Policy.

**Damaged or wrong items**
If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, email us a photo within 14 days of
delivery and we'll send a replacement at no cost.

## Subscription policy (cancellation policy)

**How Subscribe & Save works**
When you choose Subscribe & Save, you pay the discounted subscription price
today and authorize us to charge the same discounted price, plus any applicable
shipping and taxes, on each future delivery at the frequency you selected
(for example, every 4 months). Your subscription renews automatically until
you cancel.

**Price:** The subscriber price is shown on the product page and at checkout
before you pay. If the price ever changes, we'll email you at least 14 days
before your next charge.

**Skip, pause or cancel anytime**
- Log in to your account at [pawlio.com/account] → Subscriptions → Cancel,
  Skip or Pause. It takes less than a minute.
- Or email [support@pawlio.com] and we'll do it for you within 1 business day.
- To avoid your next charge, cancel before your next billing date shown in your
  account. There are no cancellation fees and no minimum number of orders.

**Reminders:** We'll email you before each renewal so you're never surprised.
[Enable upcoming-order emails in the Shopify Subscriptions app settings, or
remove this sentence.]

**Refunds on subscriptions:** Your first subscription order is covered by our
60-day Happy Dog Guarantee. Renewal orders can be refunded if you contact us
within 14 days of the renewal charge and the product hasn't shipped yet.
